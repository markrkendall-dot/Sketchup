Wesley Ave bike shed: a 12' x 8' bike shop, no permit

The biggest box North Carolina lets you build without a building permit is 12' in every direction, height included. This page puts a 12' x 8' version of it in the wedge below the Matheson Ave retaining wall and lays it out as a small bike shop: six XL 29ers hung on the back wall (2,000 mm long, 780 mm bars), a parts tower, a short bench with a shelf over it, a transom over the door, and a tower repair stand that works in the aisle with the doors open. The roof pitches 2:12 toward the wall and runs 26" past the back wall, so the 3' strip behind the shed becomes covered storage for wheelsets and long tools. Move the sliders to match your tape.

Still to confirm on site

  • Tape from the existing shed's front-right corner straight across to the retaining wall, and the gap behind the shed; set the first slider to the gap and compare the wedge readout with the tape.
  • Tape the clear wall length from the sawhorse corner toward the house; the shop needs about 12' of it starting about 9' along the string line.
  • Ask Charlotte Planning which setback applies at the Matheson right of way before fixing the 36" gap. The 12' rule is the state building code; zoning is separate.
  • While asking, confirm that a roof edge 10" off the wall face is an acceptable projection; the floor stays 12' x 8' either way, and the gutter keeps the roof's water out of the gap.
