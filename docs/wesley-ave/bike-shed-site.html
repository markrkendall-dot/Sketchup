Wesley Ave bike shed: a 12' x 12' bike shop, no permit

The biggest box North Carolina lets you build without a building permit is 12' in every direction, height included. This page sizes that box in the wedge below the Matheson Ave retaining wall and lays it out as a small bike shop: six XL 29ers hung on the back wall (2,000 mm long, 780 mm bars), wheelsets stored flat on a shelf above them, a parts tower, a bench, and room for a tower repair stand with a bike in it. Move the sliders to match your tape.
