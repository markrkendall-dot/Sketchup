Wesley Ave bike shed: a 12' x 6' bike shop, no permit

The biggest box North Carolina lets you build without a building permit is 12' in every direction, height included. This page puts a 12' x 6' version of it, cut from 8' deep on 10 November to give lawn back, in the wedge below the Matheson Ave retaining wall and lays it out as a small bike shop: six XL 29ers hung on the back wall (2,000 mm long, 780 mm bars), a parts tower, a short bench by the door, a shelf for boxes, a transom over the door, and a tower repair stand that parks inside and works out in front of the open doors. At 6' deep it stores and it has a bench, but there is no room to work on a bike inside. The roof pitches 2.8:12 toward the wall and runs 26" past the back wall, so the 3' strip behind the shed becomes covered storage for wheelsets and long tools. Move the sliders to match your tape.

Still to confirm on site

  • Tape the lawn from the house wall to the old shed's front (drawn 17.5 ft; the plat scales to 16.4 ft) and set the first slider.
  • Tape from the old shed's front-right corner square across to the retaining wall (drawn 8.9 ft) and from its back-right corner (drawn 1 ft); set the second slider until the wedge readout matches.
  • Stake the shop's four corners: back wall 3 ft off the wall face, near end 9 ft along the wall from the old shed's back corner. Walk it before buying lumber: the house-end front corner lands about 8.3 ft from the house wall.
  • The Matheson lot line is 11 to 13 ft behind the wall, so the shop sits about 15 ft inside it. Ask Charlotte Planning to confirm that no street-side rule reaches that far. The 12' rule is the state building code; zoning is separate.
  • The roof's back edge sits under the jasmine and the crepe myrtles on the bank: plan on a gutter guard, trim the vine back along the shop, and expect to prune branches over the roof. The gutter can be cleaned from the bank.
