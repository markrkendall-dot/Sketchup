Wesley Ave bike shop in 3D

The shop as it would stand along the retaining wall behind the house: tall door wall facing the yard with a transom, the roof pitched 2:12 toward the wall and carried 26" past the back wall to cover the strip behind it, six XL 29ers on Steadyracks, the bench, parts tower and stand inside, wheels and long tools hung under the overhang outside. The site was corrected on 10 November from two photos taken on the third-floor deck: the wall is inside the lot with a planted bank and the sidewalk fence behind it, the old shed's back corner nearly touches the wall, and the lawn between the house and the old shed is about 17.5 ft deep, so the shop's nearest corner is about 6.7 ft from the house.
