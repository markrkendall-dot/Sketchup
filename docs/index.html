House plans

Each plan in 3D and as floor plans. They open in the browser, on a phone, an iPad or a computer, with nothing to install. Inside a plan, its name at the top left switches to another plan or comes back here.

Looking around

3D
Drag to orbit (one finger on a touch screen), pinch or scroll to zoom, two fingers to pan. Fit 3D brings the whole house back into view. N, S, E and W show the four sides straight on; Perspective goes back to orbiting.
Existing, Proposed, All
Existing is the house as it is. Proposed is the design. All overlays the two, with work to demolish in red and new work in green.
Levels, Roofs, Furniture
Tick or untick the Basement, First Floor and Second Floor to show each level. Roofs puts the roofs on; leave it off to look down into the rooms. Furniture adds the furniture.
Plan
The floor plan of each level. Pinch or scroll to zoom, drag to pan, double-tap to reset.
