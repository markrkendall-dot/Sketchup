# 146 Mick Road: the proposed layout, v6 (grand stair hall, north band, stove, upstairs bath)

## Context

MK's brief of 14 Oct 2026, on top of the proposed design of PLAN-v4 and the kitchen of PLAN-v5:

- remove the chimney;
- put a wood stove in the Living's south-west corner, on a stainless flue instead of masonry;
- stop the kitchen about 4' short of the north wall, with a walk-in pantry in the north-east and
  the powder room to the north in that space;
- fit a washer and dryer (MK chose a closet in the master bath);
- make the front room and the Bunk over it a grand staircase open through both floors;
- add a full bath with a stall shower in the second floor's north-east.

The app had no floor opening, no stair railing and no round metal flue, so this round added all
three (Level.floorOpenings, Stairs.railings, Column kind 'flue'; DESIGN.md "Floor openings", the
"Stair railings" item and "Site and terrain").

## Constraints found while planning

- A new wall cannot meet an existing one inside an existing opening's span (demolished or not):
  the shared wall would split there and the opening would move in the Existing view. So:
  - the north band's wall is at y 51.5 (faces 49..54), its south face at the old bath door's north
    jamb (y 54): the band is 3'6" deep, not 4';
  - the powder room / pantry divider is at x 261.5 (faces 259..264), just east of the old bath's
    north window (jambs 210..258): the powder room is 7'2" wide, the pantry 3'11";
  - the upstairs bath's new wall is at y 72.5 (faces 70..75), north of the old east window (jambs
    75..121).
- Floor to floor is 108": 15 risers of 7.2"; 10.5" treads (2R + T = 24.9").
- The store's shortest flight run is 36", so the stair starts with a one-riser platform.

## Built differently from the first plan

- The band is 3'6" deep, not 4', and the bath's south wall is at y 72.5, not 83.5 (the
  constraints above).
- The powder room door is at x 200..228 (the band's wall is split at x 194.5 by the old bath
  wall's line), and its vanity is on the west wall.
- The Bunk's east wall stub (y 83.5..99.5) is demolished so the stair's top opens into the hall;
  the opening gets the extra corner for the 5" of guard there.
- The upstairs bath keeps its existing label ("Bath") and the merged hall keeps "Office / hall".
