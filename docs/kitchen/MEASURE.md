## Measured (MK, 15 and 16 Nov 2026)

Openings are inside the trim. These answer items 1 to 6, 9 to 15, 17 and most of 18, 19 and 24 below.

- Room: 13 ft (156") wide, 27'6" (330") north wall to fireplace wall, ceiling 9 ft (108").
- Walls through an opening, trim included: outside 8", pantry 6.5".
- Floor: the dark floor is paint under a damaged floor. Draw one wood floor throughout.
- Finishes today (from the photos): espresso slab cabinets with steel bar pulls, a mottled tan counter, sage-grey subway tile (to the sill on the west wall, to the wall cabinets elsewhere), pale grey-green walls, white trim, a mid-brown wood-look plank floor.
- West wall, north to south: the sink window 54.5" wide x 47" tall, sill 59" below the ceiling,
  starting 16.5" south of the pantry's wall; 48" on, the sliding door, 68" x 93"; 45" on, a
  window 34" x 55", sill 68" below the ceiling.
- North wall: a window 19.5" x 52", sill 65" below the ceiling, 23" from the north-east corner; the
  pantry's window is its mirror image, 23" from the north-west corner.
- East wall: a window 22.5" square, sill 40.5" below the ceiling, about 9 ft from the fireplace wall.
- Pantry: its east wall's kitchen face is 97.5" from the east wall (where the oven tower meets it)
  and runs 34" south from the north wall (to the door's hinge); its south wall's kitchen face, by
  the dishwasher, is 60" from the north wall. Door 28" x 81". Nothing inside that cannot move.
  MK expects to remove the pantry.
- West run: 100" long from the pantry's wall, counter 25" deep, 37" high. The north run's counter is the same.
- Oven tower: 34.5" wide.
- East run: the refrigerator's north side is 112" from the north wall. The cooktop's north edge is
  50.5" from the north wall and its south edge 25" from the refrigerator (so 36.5" wide). Gas is under it.

## Still to measure

Items 7 (no step: see Floor above), 8, 16 (answered: nothing), 20 to 23, 25 to 33, and the decisions, 34 to 37
(35 in part: the pantry can go). Also the tower's depth and height, and how far the pantry's south

## Room

1. Left wall: from the far corner (inside the pantry) to where the pantry's diagonal wall meets it, then on to the dining-side end of the cabinets, the sliding door's two edges, and the window beyond.
2. Far wall: from the pantry's stub wall (where the oven tower stands) to the right-hand corner. The full width is known: 156".
3. Right wall: from the far corner to the near side of the fridge cabinet, and on to wherever that wall ends.
4. Ceiling height (floor to ceiling). If the dining side is different, both.
5. Wall thickness at a window or the pantry door (trim edge to trim edge through the opening).
6. Which way is north (or which wall faces the street).
7. Where the dark kitchen floor meets the wood floor: distance from the far wall, and whether there is a step.
8. The room's full length, far wall to the fireplace wall, and the two openings each side of the fireplace (width, and distance from the side walls). Also the small high window past the fridge.

## Windows (they stay)

For the left window and the far window, each:

9. Distance from the nearest corner to the edge of the glass opening (inside the trim).
10. Width and height of the opening inside the trim, and trim width.
11. Floor to sill, and floor to the top of the opening.

## Pantry (may be removed)

12. Its diagonal wall: where it meets the left wall (distance from the far corner) and where it meets the short stub wall beside the oven tower; the stub's length and its distance from the left wall.
13. Length of the diagonal wall, and its thickness.
14. Door width and height, and which side it hinges.
15. The pantry window: same four numbers as items 9 to 11.
16. Anything inside that cannot move: ducts, pipes, electrical panel, attic hatch.

## Cabinets and counters as they are

17. Left run: total length, cabinet depth, counter depth, counter height, counter thickness.
18. Right run and the far-wall return: the same, plus the length of each leg of the L.
19. Oven tower: width, depth, height.
20. Upper cabinets: floor to underside, height, depth. Height of the soffit above them, if any.
21. Backsplash height (counter to the top of the tile).
22. Fridge enclosure: width, depth, height of the opening.

## Appliances and services

23. Fridge: width, depth (with and without doors), height.
24. Cooktop: width, and distance from the far corner to its centre. Gas line location if known.
25. Hood: width, floor to underside. Does it vent outside, and where does the duct go.
26. Wall oven and microwave: width of each, floor to the bottom of each.
27. Dishwasher: width, and distance from the sink.
28. Sink: width, and distance from the left-wall corner to the sink's centre (this fixes the drain).
29. Outlets and switches on each wall: rough positions are enough.
30. Supply and return vents (floor, wall or ceiling) and the thermostat or panel on the left wall.

## Lights

31. Recessed lights: how many, and each one's distance from the left wall and from the far wall.
32. Any other ceiling fixtures or the ceiling vent near the centre.

## The island cart

33. Top length, width and height (in case a design keeps it).

## What decides the designs

34. Which appliances are being kept, and the sizes of any new ones already chosen.
35. Must the sink stay under the left window? Must the cooktop stay on gas where it is?
36. Seating at an island: yes or no, and for how many.
37. Budget tier, if it should steer the options (reface, new cabinets same layout, full relayout).
