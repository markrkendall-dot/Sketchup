# Kitchen: what to measure

Reference photo: `photos/01-kitchen-from-dining.jpg` (taken from the dining side, looking in).
"Left", "right" and "far" below mean as seen in that photo. Inches are fine, to the nearest 1/4.
A rough sketch with the numbers on it, photographed, is the easiest way to send them.
More photos help too: one from the far window looking back, one of each wall square on, the pantry inside.

## Room

1. Left wall: from the dining-side end of the cabinets to the pantry's angled wall.
2. Far wall: from the pantry / oven tower to the right-hand corner. Also the full width of the room, left wall to right wall.
3. Right wall: from the far corner to the near side of the fridge cabinet, and on to wherever that wall ends.
4. Ceiling height (floor to ceiling). If the dining side is different, both.
5. Wall thickness at a window or the pantry door (trim edge to trim edge through the opening).
6. Which way is north (or which wall faces the street).
7. Where the dark kitchen floor meets the wood floor: distance from the far wall, and whether there is a step.
8. How far the room continues toward the camera (to the next wall or opening), and anything on the walls there.

## Windows (they stay)

For the left window and the far window, each:

9. Distance from the nearest corner to the edge of the glass opening (inside the trim).
10. Width and height of the opening inside the trim, and trim width.
11. Floor to sill, and floor to the top of the opening.

## Pantry (may be removed)

12. Outside footprint: how far it comes out from the left wall and from the far wall.
13. Length of the angled wall and where each end of it lands.
14. Door width and height, and which side it hinges.
15. The pantry window: same four numbers as items 9 to 11.
16. Anything inside that cannot move: ducts, pipes, electrical panel, attic hatch.

## Cabinets and counters as they are

17. Left run: total length, cabinet depth, counter depth, counter height, counter thickness.
18. Right run and the far-wall return: the same, plus the length of each leg of the L.
19. Oven tower: width, depth, height.
20. Upper cabinets: floor to underside, height, depth. Height of the soffit above them, if any.
21. Backsplash height (counter to the top of the tile).
22. Fridge enclosure: width, depth, height of the opening.

## Appliances and services

23. Fridge: width, depth (with and without doors), height.
24. Cooktop: width, and distance from the far corner to its centre. Gas line location if known.
25. Hood: width, floor to underside. Does it vent outside, and where does the duct go.
26. Wall oven and microwave: width of each, floor to the bottom of each.
27. Dishwasher: width, and distance from the sink.
28. Sink: width, and distance from the left-wall corner to the sink's centre (this fixes the drain).
29. Outlets and switches on each wall: rough positions are enough.
30. Supply and return vents (floor, wall or ceiling) and the thermostat or panel on the left wall.

## Lights

31. Recessed lights: how many, and each one's distance from the left wall and from the far wall.
32. Any other ceiling fixtures or the ceiling vent near the centre.

## The island cart

33. Top length, width and height (in case a design keeps it).

## What decides the designs

34. Which appliances are being kept, and the sizes of any new ones already chosen.
35. Must the sink stay under the left window? Must the cooktop stay on gas where it is?
36. Seating at an island: yes or no, and for how many.
37. Budget tier, if it should steer the options (reface, new cabinets same layout, full relayout).
